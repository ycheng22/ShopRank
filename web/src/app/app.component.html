Evaluation Guardrails & Conventions

  • Definition of "Relevant": Strictly defined as gain > 0 (Exact, Substitute, and Complement count towards Recall@k and MRR@k).
  • Zero-Leakage Unlabelled Queries: Queries with empty qrels are skipped and reported, never penalized as 0.
  • True Full-Corpus Retrieval: Evaluated against the entire 29,844 catalog items, not pre-filtered candidate pools. Unjudged hits receive gain 0, reflecting real-world difficulty.
